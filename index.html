01. Generate

Randomized DFS carves a maze by jumping between cells. A stack remembers the path while the algorithm backtracks at dead ends.

02. Solve

DFS searches deeply with a stack. BFS uses a queue to search by distance. Both record predecessors to reconstruct a valid path.

03. Experiment

Paint new passages and walls to make loops or unreachable areas. Compare algorithms, or play the maze yourself.
